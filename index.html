The Atelier Standards

The Story of 164 Udoumana Street

Born in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Debbie's Collection elevates children's wear and bespoke loungewear with meticulous bespoke tailoring and heirloom natural textiles.

Precision Made-to-Measure

Every bespoke garment is drafted to your specific measurements: chest, waist, shoulder-to-hem, and sleeve lengths for children and loungewear.

Hypoallergenic Luxury Fibres

We source Grade 6A Mulberry silk, organic Turkish flax linen, and GOTS-certified bamboo rayon to ensure supreme softness on delicate infant skin.

Akwa Ibom Sartorial Heritage

Integrating traditional Akwete and Aso-Oke handweaving with contemporary British and French tailoring silhouettes for weddings, dedications, and pageants.

Fast Doorstep Delivery

Express same-day bike courier in Uyo, 24-48 hours across Akwa Ibom State, and reliable priority doorstep delivery to Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and diaspora.

Atelier Fitting Convenience

Save Your Bespoke Measurements Once. Order in 1-Click.

Our digital Bespoke Measurement Book records chest, waist, height, and age for your children or yourself. When you order bespoke loungewear or heirloom gowns, our Udoumana tailors tailor directly to your saved profile.

How We Measure:

Chest / Bust Circumference under arms across fullest chest
Natural Waist Natural crease around torso above navel
Shoulder to Hem From top shoulder seam down to desired dress or robe hem
Child Age & Height Allows our pattern-cutters to adjust growth ease
